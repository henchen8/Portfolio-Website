<Text label="OVERVIEW" heading="Portable fitness, engineered end to end.">

FitBox was developed during the Management and Technology Summer Institute at the University of Pennsylvania. As technical Co-Founder, I designed the GTM strategy and MVP, combining mechanical innovations with business strategy to create a practical fitness solution for people on the go.

</Text>
</Section>

<Section reverse>
<Text label="PRODUCT DESIGN" heading="Compact, portable, and modular.">

Developed a compact, portable workout system using Onshape CAD. The mechanical design focuses on maximizing simulated weight resistance functionality while optimizing portability footprint. Key design features include:

- Modular resistance system for adjustable workout intensity
- Compact folding mechanism for ease of storage and transport
- Ergonomic grips and suction-powered attachment points

<Text label="ELECTRONICS" heading="Real-time workout feedback.">

Integrated an Arduino-based control system for sensor functionality, enabling real-time workout feedback. Key electronic features include:

- Sensor integration for rep counting and form detection
- Bluetooth connectivity for mobile app synchronization
- Dynamically adjustable resistance system
- Integrated display showing movement type, rep counts, and weight

</Text>

<Section>
<Text label="BUSINESS STRATEGY" heading="From concept to market.">

Developed a comprehensive go-to-market strategy and business plan to bring FitBox from concept to market. Key strategic elements include:

- GTM: Direct-to-consumer launch via e-commerce, leveraging social media marketing and fitness influencer partnerships for initial traction
- PMF: Targeting busy professionals and frequent travelers who want effective workouts without gym access or bulky equipment
- TAM: Global home fitness equipment market valued at $14B+
- SAM: Portable/compact fitness segment ~$2B in North America
- SOM: Initial target of $20M capturing early adopters in the urban professional demographic

</Text>
