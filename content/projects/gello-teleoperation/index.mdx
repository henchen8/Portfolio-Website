<Text label="TELEOPERATION SYSTEM" heading="A 3/8-scale, 6-DOF controller built on the iTurtle design.">

The GELLO leader arm is a 3/8-scale build of the open-source iTurtle manipulator, with 6 degrees of freedom to mirror the follower robot's joints. Each joint reports its position through a magnetic encoder, and the links themselves are custom-designed and 3D-printed rather than off-the-shelf hardware.

The encoders communicate over a shared serial bus, daisy-chained down to a USB hub at the base of the arm, which connects to the host machine over a single USB-C cable.
