<Text label="WHAT'S NEW IN V2" heading="Two changes drive everything else in this rebuild.">

V2 is a ground-up rebuild of the original Rubik's Cube robot around two changes:

- Full six-face visibility, so two Pixy2.1 cameras can see the entire cube and the robot can solve fully autonomously, start to finish.
- A custom PCB in place of the original's loose jumper-wire wiring.

Two Pixy2.1 cameras are mounted to cover all six faces at once, replacing V1's more limited camera placement and the manual face-checking it required — a prerequisite for solving fully autonomously, start to finish.
